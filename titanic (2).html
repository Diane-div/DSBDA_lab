1️⃣ Survival Rate

More passengers died than survived.

Survival rate is around 38%.

Majority of passengers did not survive.

2️⃣ Gender Played a Major Role

Female passengers had significantly higher survival rate.

Male passengers had much lower survival rate.

3️⃣ Passenger Class Impact

1st class passengers had the highest survival rate.

3rd class passengers had the lowest survival rate.

4️⃣ Fare Distribution

Fare distribution is highly right-skewed.

Most passengers paid lower fares.

Few passengers paid very high fares (outliers).

Higher fare passengers had better survival probability.

5️⃣ Age Factor

Age distribution is fairly spread.

Children had slightly better survival chances.

No strong linear relationship between age and fare.

6️⃣ Family Members Effect

Passengers traveling alone had lower survival chances.

Small families had slightly better survival rates.
